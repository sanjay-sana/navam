Navam is a calm, private baby tracker built for one thing: logging your newborn's day in the fewest taps possible — even one-handed at 3am.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.

Most baby tracker apps are bloated, ask you to make an account, and send your baby's data to their servers. Navam does the opposite. It's a fast, focused feeding and diaper log that works completely offline and keeps every entry on your device.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK
• Feeding — breastfeeding with a live nursing timer for each side (or enter minutes manually), bottle feeds with volume and contents, and pumping sessions, all kept separate.
• Diapers — log a wet, dirty, or mixed diaper in two taps, with optional colour, consistency and notes.
• Sleep — track naps and night sleep with a one-tap "asleep / awake" toggle and a running sleep timer.
• Growth — record weight and length and see them plotted against real WHO growth percentile curves.

SEE THE PATTERNS
• Today — a glanceable "next feed" countdown, daily feed and diaper counts, and your baby's current sleep state at a glance.
• Trends — daily feeds, milk intake, pumping output, wet and dirty diapers, and sleep over the last 7, 14, or 30 days.
• History — a clean timeline of every event that you can edit or delete anytime.

BUILT TO BE FAST AND CALM
• A gentle dark "nightlight" theme, designed for late-night and middle-of-the-night use.
• Smooth scroll pickers instead of fiddly keyboards, so logging is quick and one-handed.
• Local feed reminders you can turn on and tune to your baby's schedule.
• Back up and restore your data as a file whenever you like — perfect for switching phones.
• Export everything to a CSV spreadsheet to share with your pediatrician or keep your own records.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Navam works fully offline. Your baby's data never leaves your device — there are no servers, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. Nothing moves unless you move it. It's your data, on your phone, full stop.

WHO NAVAM IS FOR
Navam is made for a single caregiver tracking one baby on one device, who wants a simple, private, no-nonsense newborn tracker without accounts, subscriptions, or ads. If you want a quiet, dependable feeding and sleep log for those first months, Navam is for you.
