## Full description (max 4000)
```
Navam is a calm, private baby tracker built for one thing: logging your baby's
day in the fewest taps possible — even one-handed at 3am.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK
• Feeding — breast (live timer or manual), bottle (volume + contents), and
  pumping, kept separate.
• Diapers — wet, dirty, or mixed in two taps, with optional colour, consistency
  and notes.
• Sleep — naps and night sleep with a one-tap "asleep / awake" toggle and a
  running timer.
• Growth — weight and length, plotted against real WHO percentile curves.

SEE THE PATTERNS
• Today — a glanceable "next feed" countdown, daily counts, and current sleep
  state.
• Trends — daily feeds, intake, pumping, diapers, and sleep over 7/14/30 days.
• History — a clean timeline you can edit or delete anytime.

BUILT TO BE FAST AND CALM
• Gentle dark "nightlight" theme, designed for late-night use.
• Smooth scroll pickers instead of fiddly keyboards.
• Local feed reminders you can turn on and tune.
• Export everything to CSV whenever you like.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Navam works fully offline. Your baby's data never leaves your device — there are
no servers, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. It's your data, on your
phone, full stop.

Made for single-caregiver, single-device use.
```
