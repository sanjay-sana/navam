# Navam — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** _[fill in on publish, e.g. 1 July 2026]_

Navam is a personal baby tracker. **All of your data stays on your device.**
Navam has no account system, no servers, and does not collect, transmit, sell,
or share any personal information. This policy explains what that means.

## The short version
- Navam works **fully offline**. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
- There are **no accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no advertising, and no
  third‑party tracking SDKs**.
- Your data lives only in Navam's private storage on your phone. We (the
  developer) never receive it and cannot see it.

## What Navam stores (on your device only)
- **Baby profile** you enter: name, sex, date of birth, and optional birth
  weight/length.
- **Events you log**: feeds, diapers, sleep, and growth measurements.
- **App settings**: units, feed-reminder interval, night-time window, and
  display preferences.

All of the above is saved locally in the app's private, sandboxed storage. It is
not uploaded or backed up to any server by Navam.

## Network use
Navam does not require an internet connection to function and does not send your
data over the network. There is no analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting that
transmits personal data.

## Notifications
If you enable feed reminders, Navam schedules **local notifications** on your
device. These are generated on-device and are not sent through any server.

## Exporting your data
Navam lets you export your data as a **CSV file** using your device's share
sheet. This action is initiated by you, and the file goes only where you choose
to send or save it. Once exported, that copy is outside Navam's control and is
governed by wherever you place it.

## Children's privacy
Navam is a tool for a parent or caregiver to record information about their own
child. That information is entered by you and **never leaves your device**.
Navam does not knowingly collect personal information from children online —
in fact, it collects nothing online at all.

## Data retention and deletion
Because your data is stored only on your device:
- Using **Settings → Start over** deletes the baby profile and all logged data.
- **Uninstalling** Navam removes all of its data from your device.
- We cannot access, retain, or delete your data, because we never have it.

## Permissions
- **Notifications** — used only to show local feed reminders you opt into.
